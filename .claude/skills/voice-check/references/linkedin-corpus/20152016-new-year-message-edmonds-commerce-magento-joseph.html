2015/2016 New Year Message: Edmonds Commerce, Magento, E-Commerce and PHP

Created on 2015-12-18 11:20

Published on 2015-12-18 13:26

What a great year it has been. 2015 has seen some major strides forwards for Edmonds Commerce the company and for Magento, E-Commerce, PHP development as a whole. 

I have written a short summary of the major developments of the year. Before I go into that though, let me begin by saying a warm and heartfelt thanks to all of the great people we have worked with this year. It's been great fun working with you!

Magento

It's never boring in the world of Magento, but here are a few highlights from 2015:

Magento 2

The big news in the Magento world is the launch of Magento 2. Widely anticipated and eagerly awaited, Magento 2 has given the platform a fundamental reboot. It brings much needed things such as testability and scalability to complement the range of flexibility and features that Magento has always enjoyed. 

eBay Exit

Other major Magento news is the fact that Magento the company is no longer owned or a part of eBay. Personally I regard this as a really positive thing, I hope it is going to bring a more agile and forward thinking approach to Magento as a whole.

E-Commerce

E-Commerce continues to be major part of our retail and business economy. Increases in broadband availability and speed combined with the ubiquity of internet capable devices means that we are only ever a click or tap away from being able to do business.

Mobile 

Google made a real point this year around the fact that sites need to be mobile friendly. Today, that means responsive and fast loading websites that have a design that adapts to any screen size without redirecting customers to alternative versions of your site. Mobile commerce is on the up and it's more important than ever that your web site is mobile friendly.

Platforms

Whilst Magento (and Magento 2) continue to be the big players in E-Commerce, there are other platforms that are still worth looking at. Also some exciting new developments.

Opencart and Prestashop continue to be the established alternatives to Magento. A new contender that is still in development but looking very promising is Sylius which is based on Symfony and brings the latest in PHP technology to an E-Commerce platform.

The other big thing developing is Oro. From, among others, Yoav Kutner who was part of the original Magento team, Oro have developed a ground breaking CRM system. They have also as part of this developed Oro BAP (Business Application Platform) on which the CRM system has been built. This BAP allows Oro and other developers to create rich web apps that are ideal for business platforms. A significant project based upon this is Akeneo which is a PIM (Product Information Management) system. If you manage your product data and are not currently using a PIM then I suggest you take a look. It's the future.

PHP

I love PHP and I have had the pleasure of seeing it develop from something quite basic and, (dare I say it, a little bit amateur ) into something that is indisputably an Enterprise Ready language for web development.

PHP 7 Released

The biggest news in the world of PHP is the release of PHP 7. This brings huge performance improvements to the language along with some other features and enhancements that will allow us to create ever more peformant, reliable and scalable PHP systems.

Edmonds Commerce

Our big news of this year was our move to new offices. Moving is never easy however we are now properly settled in and enjoying our new office space. It is much more peaceful as it's well away from the road and instead backs on the Liverpool/Leeds canal that runs through Shipley.

Along with the office move, we have given ourselves a massive upgrade in internet access. We now have a full speed leased line, the kind often used by hosting companies or very large offices. This is absolute top notch in terms of internet access and is allowing us to work more quickly and efficiently than ever before. It's also very geeky but we love it.

 

 

We've also gained some great people, customers and team members, this year. We hope to work with even more great people next year.

That's all now, the year has come to a close and it's time to enjoy some family relaxation time and get ready for an even better year in 2016!
