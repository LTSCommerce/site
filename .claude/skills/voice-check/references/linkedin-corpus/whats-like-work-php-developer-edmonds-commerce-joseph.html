What's it like to work as a PHP Developer with Edmonds Commerce

Created on 2016-08-25 10:14

Published on 2016-08-25 10:45

Currently we are looking to hire one or two new developers to join our small but focused team at Edmonds Commerce in Shipley, near Leeds and Bradford.

Our focus as a company is purely on PHP web development for e-commerce.

These days that means we do a lot of Magento work, largely because its the most popular open source E-Commerce platform at the moment. In truth, we work with all kinds of PHP platforms and frameworks including creating totally bespoke solutions.

I'm certain that we offer a really positive, enjoyable and rewarding experience for developers that work with us and I was trying to think of the best way I can communicate that to local developers who might consider joining us.

In the end, the answer was pretty simple, just ask the team.

I sent a message on Slack and asked for a one liner. Here are three nice responses that I thought were worth quoting in this article:

Ken gave me a nicely verbose answer:

"The work is always varied, with big projects being interspersed with small ones to mix things up. The best thing is the environment, where we have a great balance between the ability to focus on our own work, while working as a team to play to our strengths and review each others' progress. The way the company invests in us as developers benefits us, the company and our clients."

Cheers Ken, that's definitely a nice quote to throw into this article.

 

Ben on the other hand was a bit more terse, but equally positive:

"A new challenge every day with a team ready to help you succeed"

Yes Ben, it is a bit cheesy, sounds like the kind of thing you'd expect written under a motivational poster

Not to be outdone by Ben's frugal use of text, Ross came up with an even shorter phrase. Utilising the rhetoric rule of three to great effect we have:

Challenging, Flexible, and Fun

I think that's a pretty good summary. We do tend to work on challenging projects. Agencies and E-Commerce business will often use us as a technical back stop to their in house resources. The most technical stuff is escalated across to us and we really quite enjoy that.

By flexible I think Ross is mainly referring to the flexi time approach that allows you to basically work when you would like to for as long as you would like (within reasonable parameters). 

Fun speaks for itself, we do tend to work on interesting and challenging projects and if you are into that kind of thing, it's great fun!

You can read about some of the stuff we do as developers on our github blog

All of our developers are Zend Certified Engineers (ZCE) and if you don't already have this qualification then we will help you to achieve it. Generally its not too challenging for an experienced PHP developer and just requires a bit of revision to round out your knowledge before sitting the exam.

Personally I really like this qualification because it is purely focused on the language itself along with some relevant general web knowledge (eg what is a 301). By having this qualification you can be assured that you are a proper PHP developer and can handle any PHP project you might be given.

Within your first year we will also expect you to sit your Magento Certified Developer (MCD) exam. This is obviously a Magento specific qualification, though it has real value in ensuring you have a complete understanding of the platform and the process of studying for the exam allows you to gain a comprehensive understanding of how Magento ticks. It is undoubtedly more challenging than the ZCE exam though its something we will help you to achieve.

As a company we are totally devoted to Linux and use it for everything desktops upwards. We've settled on using Fedora as our distribution of choice for the desktop. Largely this is due to the fact that most servers we deal with these days are running either RedHat or CentOS and so Fedora is the obvious choice as all three are very similar and share a common ancestry.
