Why you should work with us at Edmonds Commerce

Created on 2014-07-28 15:09

You are the kind of person who loves computers, loves coding and problem solving. Often you spend time at home learning new techniques. You may well have a home server running Owncloud and a VPN and a web server for your own purposes.

Your day job at an agency is OK but to be honest you would prefer something a little more challenging. However challenging projects quickly become stressful when you don't have someone in the office you can turn to when you get stuck.

Working in an office full of like minded developers who have the experience and patience required to help you out when you have issues means that you are free to push further and harder than you would do normally. There is such a huge amount of knowledge to be gained and the only way to do it is to get in there and start working with it, knowing we have your back.

Another great reason to come to Edmonds Commerce is that we are really quite flexible. We appreciate that not everyone is a morning person and that lifestyles and other commitments mean that your preferred start time might vary quite significantly. For this reason we have really quite generous flexi-time conditions that suite everyone apart from the full blown nocturnal.

Working with us you will go onto a clear path of training and certification that ensures you are moving forwards with your career. Also as you journey along this path, each achievement is also rewarded with a boost in your wage which is an excellent bit of carrot motivation.

Finally it would be the fact that we are nice people. We also make a concerted effort to work with customers that are also nice people and therefore we are able to effectively collaborate with our customers to achieve great things.

Come and join us - you won't regret it!
