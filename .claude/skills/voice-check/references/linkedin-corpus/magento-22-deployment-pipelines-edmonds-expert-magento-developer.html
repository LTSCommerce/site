Pipeline deployment,

One really nice feature that is quite subtle really but opens up a lot of possibilities is the move to what they are calling "Pipeline Deployment". Essentially what this means is that the maximum amount of work around deploying updates can be done before any changes are made to your live site. This in turn means that downtime can be kept to the barest minimum, essentially unnoticeable for most store owners.

From reading the documentation you would be forgiven for thinking this is really complex, however it doesn't really need to be. On a well powered server, there can simply be a "build area" where any new updates are automatically prepared before finally being applied the live production site. Ideally the whole process can be fully automated which means that developers find it easy and fluid to push out regular updates and you can enjoy rapid iterations on new features and code updates on your store.

Upgrading to Magento 2 .2 from 2.x

There are some backwards incompatible changes being brought in which, especially if you have custom modules or functionality then you may need to refactor. Apart from that though, the upgrade should be relatively painless. It goes without saying that upgrades should be handled by your developers and then properly tested before going into production.

Upgrading to Magento 2.2 from Magento 1

If you are still on Magento 1 then I continue to believe that the time has come to start at least preparing your upgrade to Magento 2. The powers that be in Magento have declared that Magento 1 still has at least 18 months of support, however I'd regard that as a comfortable amount of time to handle your upgrade. Don't leave it to the last minute!
