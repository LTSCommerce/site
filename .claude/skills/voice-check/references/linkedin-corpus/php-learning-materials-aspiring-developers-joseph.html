PHP Learning Materials for Aspiring Developers

Created on 2017-08-17 09:18

In my position as technical director of Edmonds Commerce, I have the unique and fortunate position to have interviewed a lot of PHP developers. I often see people who are clearly gifted and enthusiastic but lack the breadth and depth of knowledge and skill that we regard as a baseline for being able to join our team.

Not a Failure, an Opportunity

I always try to make a point when I see people like this to try encourage them to regard my turning down their application not as a failure, but as a great learning opportunity and something that has allowed them to see what they need to do in order to get where they want to be.

I have absolutely no issues with someone in this situation reapplying in a few months once they think that they have managed to get themselves up to speed. I encourage it.

Why have a Baseline?

The issue isn't that we expect people to be experts when they join us, it's that we have such a lot to teach new developers that we have to have a solid baseline or they are just going to flounder trying to progress through a vertical learning curve.

Our baseline has been set at being at or near (lets say 80%) the level where they would pass the Zend Certified Engineer exam given a bit of time to revise. That means that they need a solid understanding of all the core PHP stuff including basics, functions, object oriented, database and so on. This means that to pass the exam, they will only really need to revise on the more esoteric aspects of PHP.

How to Improve

For developers that want to improve, I have a few links that I refer them to and I thought it would be useful to post these here:

Free Online Resources

Zend Certified Engineer

This is the details of the ZCE qualification and lists all of the topics that are covered

PHP Docs

The PHP docs have to be the first port of call for learning about things such as SPL, Classes and Objects and everything else. It is the primary source of information on the language itself. Just ignore the comments because some are very old and many are not very good.

PHP The Right Way

This site contains a PHP community based reference for PHP best practice. It remains up to date and and describes modern best practices.

Stack Overflow PHP Docs

This is a bit of a fledgling effort but is worth having a look through, there are some useful bits of info in there.

Paid for Resources

Laracasts

Whilst being primarily focused on Laravel, there are lots of good general PHP development topics covered. This is not a free resource, but it is not hugely expensive and can be taken on for a month or two only.

Official Study Guide

For studying for the exam, the official study guide doesn't break the bank and is probably worth getting a copy of.

Get Out There

Writing your own code or working on legacy code bases that might be of questionable quality can really hold you back as a developer. When working on your own code, you will stick to the moves that you know and will simply not use things that you do not understand or are not even aware of.

As a developer, you need to break out of your bubble and in so doing, learn much more about PHP and web development as a whole.

Thankfully there are huge opportunities to do that these days thanks to the massive amount of top quality, open source PHP systems, platforms and libraries out there.

I'd suggest starting off with looking at highly regarded frameworks such as Symfony, Zend Framework and Laravel. I'd also suggest taking a look at platforms such as Magento, Magento 2 and Sylius.

Once you have done some reading and poking around then you can start to contribute, simply pick a library and look at the open issues and see if there is anything you fancy having a go at.

This way you will not only improve your PHP knowledge but will also learn about working with Git, Continuous Integration, Unit Testing and all kinds of other great things that are in the world of modern PHP development.

Conclusion

I am not sure there has ever been such a great opportunity for self motivated individuals to access a wealth of free training materials and opportunities that can be utilised to propel them into a financially, emotionally and intellectually rewarding career path.

And when you have got yourself up to the ZCE level - then definitely get in touch with me to talk about joining us at Edmonds Commerce!
