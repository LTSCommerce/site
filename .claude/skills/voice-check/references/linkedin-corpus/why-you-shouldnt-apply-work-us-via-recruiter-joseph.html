Why You Shouldn't Apply to work with us via a Recruiter

Created on 2017-12-07 14:32

Published on 2017-12-07 15:11

I'm reluctantly having to write this message to any current and future candidates who have or would like to apply to work with us at Edmonds Commerce


Please don't apply via a recruiter, you have a much higher chance of being hired if you apply direct


If you apply to us via a recruiter here is how it goes:

  1. Your recruiter sends us your CV
  2. We have to agree to their terms and conditions (often very long, complex and one sided)
  3. We have to agree to pay them a large percentage of your total annual salary if we hire you. Let's call it £5,000 minimum (ouch!)
  4. If we ever want to hire you, we will have to regard you as coming from the recruiter and will have to pay their fee
  5. We can never hire you directly without running the risk of being pursued legally by the recruitment company.

What does "Pursued Legally by the Recruitment Company" mean?

This means that the recruitment company will unleash their team of lawyers on us and use the courts to try to force us to pay not only their fee, but also legal costs and anything else they can come up with. The potential costs to us if we lose are really quite high.

It's a risk we would much rather not take.


On the other hand,

If you apply to work with us directly, it goes like this:

  1. You get in touch
  2. We deal with you directly
  3. When thinking on whether to hire you, we can work on the basis that there is £5,000 budget to be spent on training you up. This makes you much more attractive because you do not necessarily have to be at the level we are ultimately looking for, you just need to demonstrate that given training you can get there.
  4. Everything is so much easier, simpler and cheaper that we are much more likely to hire you


If You Previously Applied Direct but are now Using a Recruiter

If you have already applied to us directly, but then your CV is sent to us via a recruiter then we now have to treat you as coming to us through a recruiter to avoid the risk of being pursued legally by the recruiter. The only way out of this is if we spot the fact we are already talking to you at the time the recruiter first contacts us.

This means that you have probably gone from being hire-able to not being hire-able.

Also, we can never discuss hiring you again without risking being pursued legally by the recruiter.


If You Applied via Recruiter

If you have already applied to us via a recruiter then all is not necessarily lost. Many recruiters are fine and we are happy to deal with them. You are a less attractive candidate due to the extra cost, but that isn't to say we won't hire you, it's just a bit less likely.

However, if you have applied via a recruiter that we do not deal with for whatever reason(s), then it is a dead end. If we have chosen not to deal with a specific recruiter then you are now lost to us, we won't hire you via your recruiter but neither can we hire you directly without risking being pursued legally by the recruiter. It's a messy situation.


So In Conclusion:

If you apply via a recruiter, you are a much more expensive option than a candidate who applies direct. That means that we are much more likely to hire a direct applicant.

  • If you haven't already applied: apply directly
  • If you have have applied directly: don't let recruiters submit you again
  • If you have already applied via recruiter: good luck with your application!


Cheers
