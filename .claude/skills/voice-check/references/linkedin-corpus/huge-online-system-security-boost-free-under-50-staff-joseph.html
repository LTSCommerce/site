Work from home has really changed the game for a lot of people. In IT it has meant that things that previously could be locked down behind office firewalls and static IPs have needed to be opened up to allow people to work from home.

For online/ecommerce businesses then this generally include one or more online admin systems that allow people to do the work of the business. Access to these systems is hugely valuable and must be protected. Actually achieving this protection can be a little tricky though.

For example most e-commerce systems like Magento have an admin area where the user has powerful control and access to the website configuration and data.

Traditional VPN Approach

The first thing many people turned to is the traditional VPN. This allows remote workers to connect to the office network from their home. It routes some or all of their internet traffic via the office and that way, any online systems that are restricted to office IPs or are behind the office firewall can be accessed.

The challenge with this approach is that builds in a single point of failure and it puts a burden of maintenance on in house office staff that may not really be up to it. Finally it relies on the office having a very good and stable internet connection which, sadly, is not always the case in the UK. VPNs fall over, get congested, have issues and can generally be a bit of a PITA.

A Better Way

Recently we've rolled out an alternative strategy to one of our clients and I'm happy to say it was really successful. The system allows online systems/admins to be protected at the same or even stronger levels of protection than can be achieved with a VPN but without any of the pain that a VPN generally brings.

Access to the system can be based upon one click logins via Google Workplace, Okta, Azure AD, Facebook, Linkedin and more, which makes it very easy for users to access things.

You can control access and allow/deny to specific users or whole groups based upon a variety of criteria, all managed via an easy to use online dashboard.

Further security can be added by implementing mTLS - mutual SSL security which requires the user to have a certificate installed on their PC or device. This password protected certificate can be installed by administrators which then gives extra control on preventing unexpected or unauthorised access to online systems.

Its Really Free (For less than 50 users)

The cost of the system is literally nothing as long as you have less than 50 users. You can pay for a premium tier which becomes a requirement for greater than 50 users, but below that user count, you do not need to subscribe unless there is a feature you particularly want.

The effort required to set up the system is something in the region of 10 hours of technical work and then you will need to have time to configure all the users etc which can be done by yourselves via the online dashboard, or of course it can be done for you.

The service is provided by a major blue chip provider and so there is no worries about this being something dodgy. They are just so massive that they can offer this service for free to smaller players and I guess they hope you will end up using other services of theirs that you will want to pay for.
