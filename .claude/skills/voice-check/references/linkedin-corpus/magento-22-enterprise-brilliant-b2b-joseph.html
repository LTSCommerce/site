I wrote previously about one of the nice features coming in with Magento 2.2 for Community and Enterprise editions which is the deployment pipelines.

In this article, I'm going to focus on one of the most compelling and appropriate Enterprise features that is being added to Magento 2.2 - Business to Business

In contrast to Business to Consumer (B2C), the requirements of B2B e-commerce are a bit more complex. Things like pricing become flexible, payment is often on account and there are lots of other ways that a B2B relationship can become more complex.

With Magento 2.2 Enterprise, some of these requirements are now being met head on and with a certain amount of style.

Companies

We now have the concept of Companies to which one or many customer accounts can belong to. There is a hierarchy of company roles meaning that some company users might be able to place orders whereas others might be more read only. You can read the full set of roles here. For anyone selling into a large organisation this is a great feature as it really allows a highly customised configuration to be set up on a per company account basis.

Credit

An essential B2B feature, the credit modules allow company members to make purchases on account, manage the credit balance and also to manage the reimbursement of those credit purchases. You can see the full process flow here.

Shared Catalog

This feature will be particularly applicable to those kinds of organisation who provide bespoke goods and services on a B2B basis, for example printers providing marketing materials to retail outlets.

A custom shared catalog is a private set of products and categories that are only visible to the customers from the company that has been configured. This allows them to then purchase these custom products on account as described above. I can really see this being a killer feature for those that need it.

Negotiable Quotes

For those companies that sell in highly price competitive or volatile markets where price negotiation is a fundamental part of the B2B sales flow, this feature will be perfect.

Allowing customers from companies to create their own quote requirements by adding items to the cart and then for the price to be negotiated and confirmed before the customer then completes the order and pays the agreed amount.

You can see the process flow here

Brilliant B2B

The above features are of course in addition to all of the standard Magento 2.2 functionality but they really add a whole new dimension of possibilities. By being offered as part of core Enterprise rather than something bolted in with third party extensions, you can rely on them being fully supported in the long term.
