PHP Developer Profile for Recruitment

Created on 2017-04-10 16:59

Published on 2017-04-10 18:05

I wanted to write something to assist the recruiters that we are working with to understand exactly the kind of developer we are looking for at Edmonds Commerce

Our attitude to hiring is entirely inclusive and based purely on the merit of the individual.

PHP Competence

The first thing that we care about is competence with PHP.

By this we mean core PHP skills - the skill and knowledge of the PHP language itself.

We do not require new developers to already know the frameworks, platforms or tools that we work with, as all of that will be taught.

There is a qualification that measures exactly the kind of skill we are looking for - its called Zend Certified Engineer. The exam covers the length and breadth of the PHP language and to pass this exam, a developer must have a well rounded knowledge of the full language.

For a developer who already knows PHP well then passing the exam should not require much more than a few evenings study. There are loads of online resources to help developers study and practice for the exam, including this online practice exam, however some of the information is not completely up to date with the latest PHP 7 based qualification.

A successful candidate for our PHP developer roles will be at a level where they would pass the ZCE exam with a small amount of revision.

Unfortunately some recruiters have sent us candidates that have only very limited PHP skills and its both a waste of time and a cause of real dismay when a candidate comes for interview and does not really know PHP.

Fast Learning rather than Extensive Knowledge

Based on the fact that a successful candidate may know PHP well but might not know anything about platforms we work with, such as Magento, then it's clear that an ability to learn quickly and effectively is a crucial skill. I'm not aware of another industry that has such a strong requirement for constant and in depth learning.

Learning can be hard work and a new developer will have a lot to learn, and learn quickly so there really needs to be a strong willingness and motivation.

A huge bank of knowledge is of course useful, but we will always choose someone who can learn quickly over someone who has lots of knowledge and experience but is less willing to learn.

We will provide all the help we can, but when it comes to this kind of learning challenge, the person really has to motivate themselves to get through it.

Team Players, Not Rockstars

A developer at Edmonds Commerce is part of a great team. And that means they also need to be a great team player.

We are really looking for people who are confident enough to provide input to group discussions, but humble enough to admit when they are wrong.

They need to have the confidence to ask for help when they are stuck and be willing to help others in turn.

Flexible Working

One of the things that our developers appreciate the most about working here is that things are flexible and relaxed.

Prefer to roll in at 11am after they've had chance to properly wake up? That's absolutely fine, in fact it is encouraged.

Would they rather get in at 7:30am and enjoy hours of quiet productivity and then get to leave early and spend time with their family - great, carry on!

The focus is on doing good work and developers are free to work in the way that is best for them.

That means that the kind of developer that will work with us is someone that enjoys this kind of freedom. The caveat is that with this kind of freedom, they do need to have a certain degree of self motivation in order to achieve a good output of work.

Permanent

When someone joins our team, we expect it to be anything between 3-12 months before they are fully up to speed.

When we provide a solution to our customers, we might be supporting that solution for many years.

We will do our very best to ensure that a developer who joins our team remains absolutely happy to continue working with us for as long as possible.

This means that contractors are really not a good fit for us, its permanent all the way.

Local

Call us old fashioned, but we really think that a team that is physically together is going to work better. We get to go out for lunch together, we can go for drinks after work sometimes or celebrate each others birthdays.

When it comes to work, we are able to very easily collaborate, assist, advise or ask for help. We can do real daily standups. We can communicate very easily and really believe communication is absolutely key in providing the best service to our clients.

Our office provides a quiet environment, powerful PCs with many screens, a fully uncontended leased line to give us exceptionally good internet access. These kinds of things are very hard to do in a home situation.

This means that a successful candidate will either already be in our area, or be willing to relocate.

We are lucky enough to be in a very low cost of living area in comparison to other parts of the country. That means that someone relocating up from somewhere like London can expect to be able to afford a much nicer living space and have a greatly increased ability to purchase the finer things in life or build up their savings.

Summary

So in summary, we are looking for someone

  • who is either local or willing to move,
  • highly competent with PHP
  • and willing to learn other things quickly,
  • a great team player
  • and will appreciate a laid back, productivity focused attitude to working.
