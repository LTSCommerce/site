Who are Edmonds Commerce? Outcomes from our first Growth Accelerator Session

Created on 2014-08-21 12:10

Published on 2014-08-21 13:23

We have just started going through the Growth Accelerator process which should result in us developing a well put together 3 year plan to grow the business and allow us to achieve our aspirations and goals.

This first meeting has been a structured and collaborative brainstorming session with the whole team to establish our strengths and really what makes us different.

The Process

The process was really interesting and it was great to see a real consensus amongst the team as to exactly who Edmonds Commerce are and what makes us different. I thought this might be really valuable for clients and partners (both existing and potential) to get an insight into Edmonds Commerce as a company and what we can do for you.

Each person was given a stack of yellow hexagons and a marker pen with which to brain storm their ideas of our various strengths. These were then placed onto the magnetic white board.

We then worked together to group these individual cards into groups so that we can establish which particular themes are coming through.

The final task was to give a general heading for each group of strengths. These general headings were written up on a green hexagon placed next to the group of yellow hexagons.

Here is a quick run through the headings we came up with:

Confidence with Complexity


This heading illustrates how as a company we embrace complex projects and problems. We are definitely not afraid of taking on challenging tasks or problem solving, in fact we really enjoy this kind of thing.

Commercial Management


This one is about our project management and quoting. We have gained a lot of experience over the years and this translates to a pragmatic, realistic and pretty accurate quoting and project delivery process.

Holistic Knowledge of Server


We struggled to come up with a good general heading for this one. We were trying to illustrate the fact that we are not just PHP developers but are also more than happy to work on the web server infrastructure as a whole, from the Linux operating system level right up to the PHP and web server level. The current buzz word term for this is "full stack developer".

Knowledge and Experience


For this heading we are talking about primarily our expertise and knowledge with Magento and the fact that we have been working with it for years (since it was born really) which is a real strength.

High Ethical Standard


For this heading we were talking about the fact that we work as a business as honestly and with the utmost integrity we can. This means that we will always try to be realistic and honest with clients about what can be done and what challenges we are facing when working with our clients on their projects.

Commitment to Learning

For this heading we were talking about our philosophy of ensuring that our whole team is constantly learning, gaining qualifications such as the ZCE and MCE exams and generally regarding personal development and training of our team as a first class priority.

Flexible and Adaptive


For this heading we discussed our general flexibility. This ranges from the flexitime that our team enjoys in terms of working hours which has the knock on effect that we are well placed to perform client tasks outside their normal business hours, through to our willingness to get our hands dirty on projects that are not really in an ideal situation.

High Quality Standard

In this final heading we talked about our reputation for quality and the fact that we focus on being a quality Magento agency. We always try to follow best practices and standards in our work and generally focus on providing the highest levels of quality for our clients.
