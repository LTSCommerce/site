To contract, or sub contract, that is the question

Created on 2015-03-13 12:22

Published on 2015-03-13 12:22

OK maybe not quite as dramatic as Hamlet's contemplation of the ultimate solution to his suffering but it is an important question none the less.

The question I am asking is one that I have just discussed with a potential new client of ours who is weighing up the options of bringing in a contract developer to supplement their in house team on a new Magento project, or to sub contract the requirements to us.

My advice is of course entirely biased however I am honest when I say that I consider the risks of using a contractor to be much greater than the risks of outsourcing to a reputable and professional agency such as ourselves.

In terms of getting the work actually done, as long as the chosen contractor is up to standard then I would assume that the outcome will largely by the same with either approach. The difference begins when the project is delivered.

Unless you have an ongoing requirement, chances are you will be wishing your contractor well in his new role somewhere far flung from your office once the project is launched. Then a week later the phone rings and your client has discovered a strange and complex but critical issue. The only person who truly understands exactly how this specific custom feature was implemented is now absent and you are left trying to reassure your client that you will resolve this issue promptly whilst wondering how the hell you are going to do that.

You may at that point call a professional Magento agency such as ourselves, and we will gladly help you out. However despite the fact that we really know what we are doing, we are coming in cold and the odds are certainly stacked higher against us than if we had implemented the feature initially.

The alternative situation is that we are very familiar with the specifics of your client requirements and the precise way those requirements were satisfied with bespoke development - because we did it. We know exactly where the problem is likely to lie and can quickly resolve the issue. Within minutes of your client call, the issue is resolved and everyone gets on with their day.

Just some food for thought as you consider how you are going to resource your next complex Magento build.
